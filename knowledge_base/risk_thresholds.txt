RISK THRESHOLDS DOCUMENT
Agentic AI Loan Approval System — Version 1.0
==============================================

1. DEBT-TO-INCOME (DTI) THRESHOLDS

DTI is calculated as: monthly_existing_liabilities / (annual_income / 12)

1.1 DTI Risk Bands
- Low Risk:    DTI <= 0.30 (30%)   — Strong repayment capacity
- Medium Risk: DTI 0.31 – 0.45    — Adequate repayment capacity
- High Risk:   DTI 0.46 – 0.60    — Stretched repayment capacity; review required
- Critical:    DTI > 0.60 (60%)   — Automatic rejection; insufficient capacity

1.2 DTI Adjustments by Employment Type
- Government/PSU employees: DTI threshold raised by 0.05 (job security premium)
- Contract workers: DTI threshold lowered by 0.05 (income variability)
- Unemployed: DTI calculated on declared alternate income only; max DTI 0.20

2. COMPOSITE RISK SCORE

The composite risk score (0–100) integrates credit, DTI, and employment factors.

2.1 Score Interpretation
- 0–39:   Low Risk    → APPROVED (subject to policy compliance)
- 40–69:  Medium Risk → REVIEW_REQUIRED (human underwriter decision)
- 70–100: High Risk   → REJECTED

2.2 Score Component Weights
- Credit Score Component:    40% of total risk score
- DTI Component:             35% of total risk score
- Employment Stability:      15% of total risk score
- Loan-to-Income Ratio:      10% of total risk score

3. HARD REJECTION TRIGGERS

The following conditions result in immediate REJECTED classification
regardless of other scores:
- Credit score < 500
- DTI > 0.60
- Age < 18 or > 70
- Active bankruptcy or insolvency proceedings (if declared)
- Loan amount < INR 10,000 or > INR 10,000,000

4. MANDATORY REVIEW TRIGGERS

The following conditions escalate to REVIEW_REQUIRED even if risk score
would otherwise suggest APPROVED:
- Credit score 500–549 (Poor band)
- DTI 0.50–0.60 (borderline high)
- Thin credit file (< 3 credit accounts)
- Recent credit default in past 24 months
- Self-employed with < 2 years in business
- Loan amount > INR 2,500,000 (high-value manual check)
- Loan-to-income ratio > 3x annual income (unsecured)
