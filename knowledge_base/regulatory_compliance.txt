REGULATORY COMPLIANCE DOCUMENT
Agentic AI Loan Approval System — Version 1.0
==============================================

1. FAIR LENDING REQUIREMENTS

1.1 Non-Discrimination Policy
Loan decisions must be based exclusively on financial and creditworthiness
criteria. Decisions must not be influenced by:
- Race, religion, caste, or ethnicity
- Gender or marital status
- Geographic location (except for legally permitted area-based risk pricing)
- Age (within the eligible range of 18–70 years)
- Disability status

1.2 Equal Credit Opportunity
All applications must receive a decision within 30 days of submission.
Rejected applicants must be provided with the specific reasons for rejection
upon request.

2. KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Mandatory KYC Documents
Before disbursement (manual process, outside this system):
- Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, or Voter ID)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or rental agreement)
- PAN Card (mandatory for all loans above INR 50,000)

2.2 KYC Verification
This AI system flags applications for manual KYC verification when:
- Declared address does not match application location
- Applicant age < 25 (enhanced due diligence)
- Loan amount exceeds INR 1,000,000

3. AUDIT AND RECORD RETENTION

3.1 Audit Trail Requirements
A complete audit record must be maintained for every loan application
processed by this system, including:
- All input fields provided by the applicant
- Intermediate agent assessments (profile, risk, policy)
- Final decision with confidence score and explanation
- Unique Case ID
- Processing timestamp (UTC)

3.2 Retention Period
Audit records must be retained for a minimum of 7 years from the date of
the decision. Records for approved loans must be retained for 7 years
after loan closure.

4. MODEL GOVERNANCE

4.1 AI Decision Explainability
The system must provide a human-readable explanation for every decision.
The explanation must reference specific financial factors (DTI, credit score,
income) rather than opaque algorithmic outputs.

4.2 Human Override
Any decision classified as REVIEW_REQUIRED must be reviewed and approved
or rejected by a licensed human underwriter before disbursement.
The AI system's recommendation is advisory only in REVIEW_REQUIRED cases.

4.3 Model Monitoring
Decision distributions (approval rate, average confidence score, rejection
reasons) must be reviewed monthly to detect model drift or bias.
