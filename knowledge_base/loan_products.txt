LOAN PRODUCTS DOCUMENT
Agentic AI Loan Approval System — Version 1.0
==============================================

1. PERSONAL LOANS

1.1 Standard Personal Loan
- Amount Range: INR 10,000 to INR 2,500,000
- Tenure: 6 to 60 months
- Minimum Credit Score: 650
- Eligible Employment: Salaried, Government, Self-employed (>= 2 years)
- Interest Rate Band: 10.5% – 24% per annum (based on risk profile)

1.2 Premium Personal Loan
- Amount Range: INR 500,000 to INR 5,000,000
- Tenure: 12 to 84 months
- Minimum Credit Score: 750 (Excellent band)
- Eligible Employment: Salaried with >= 2 years in current organisation
- Interest Rate Band: 9.5% – 14% per annum

1.3 Micro Personal Loan
- Amount Range: INR 10,000 to INR 200,000
- Tenure: 3 to 24 months
- Minimum Credit Score: 500 (Poor band minimum)
- Eligible Employment: All types
- Interest Rate Band: 18% – 36% per annum

2. HOME LOANS

2.1 Residential Home Loan
- Amount Range: INR 500,000 to INR 10,000,000
- Tenure: 12 to 360 months (up to 30 years)
- Minimum Credit Score: 700
- Eligible Employment: Salaried and Self-employed
- Maximum Loan-to-Value (LTV): 80%
- Interest Rate Band: 8.5% – 11% per annum

3. BUSINESS LOANS

3.1 SME Business Loan
- Amount Range: INR 200,000 to INR 5,000,000
- Tenure: 12 to 60 months
- Minimum Credit Score: 650
- Eligible Employment: Self-employed, Business Owner
- Business Age Requirement: >= 2 years
- Interest Rate Band: 12% – 22% per annum

4. AUTO LOANS

4.1 Vehicle Loan
- Amount Range: INR 100,000 to INR 2,500,000
- Tenure: 12 to 84 months
- Minimum Credit Score: 600
- Eligible Employment: All except Unemployed
- Maximum Loan-to-Value: 85%
- Interest Rate Band: 9% – 15% per annum
