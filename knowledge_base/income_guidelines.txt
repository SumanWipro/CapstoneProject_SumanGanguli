INCOME GUIDELINES DOCUMENT
Agentic AI Loan Approval System — Version 1.0
==============================================

1. MINIMUM INCOME REQUIREMENTS

1.1 Absolute Minimum
The minimum annual income to qualify for any loan product is INR 150,000.
Applications from individuals earning below this threshold are rejected.

1.2 Income by Employment Type
- Salaried (private sector): Minimum INR 250,000 per annum
- Salaried (government/PSU): Minimum INR 200,000 per annum
- Self-employed professional: Minimum INR 400,000 per annum (net profit)
- Self-employed business owner: Minimum INR 500,000 per annum (net profit)
- Contract/freelance worker: Minimum INR 350,000 per annum (average last 2 years)

2. INCOME VERIFICATION

2.1 Required Documentation (for manual review cases)
- Salaried: Last 3 months salary slips and Form 16 / ITR last 2 years
- Self-employed: ITR for last 3 years, CA-certified P&L statement
- Contract: Bank statements for last 12 months plus client contracts

2.2 Income Stability Assessment
Income is considered stable when:
- Salaried: Employed with current employer for >= 6 months
- Self-employed: Business operational for >= 2 years
- Contract: Same primary client for >= 12 months OR multiple clients

3. LOAN-TO-INCOME RATIO

3.1 The maximum loan amount is capped at:
- 3x annual income for unsecured personal loans
- 5x annual income for home loans and mortgage-backed loans
- 2x annual income for short-term loans (tenure < 12 months)

3.2 Applications exceeding these ratios are automatically classified as
REVIEW_REQUIRED regardless of credit score.

4. INCOME CONSISTENCY CHECKS

4.1 Red flags that trigger REVIEW_REQUIRED:
- Declared income significantly lower than average for the occupation/location
- Sudden income increase of more than 50% in a single year
- Multiple income sources without documentation
- Income declared in cash-based businesses above INR 1,000,000
