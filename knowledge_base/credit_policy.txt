CREDIT POLICY DOCUMENT
Agentic AI Loan Approval System — Version 1.0
==============================================

1. CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS

1.1 Minimum Acceptable Credit Score
All loan applicants must have a minimum CIBIL credit score of 500 to be
considered for any loan product. Applications with a score below 500 are
automatically rejected without further review.

1.2 Credit Score Bands
The institution classifies credit scores into four bands:
- Excellent: 750–900. Applicants in this band qualify for the best interest
  rates and the highest loan amounts.
- Good: 650–749. Applicants qualify for standard loan products with
  competitive rates. Minor risk adjustments may apply.
- Fair: 550–649. Applicants may qualify for limited loan products. Stricter
  DTI requirements apply. A co-applicant or collateral may be required.
- Poor: 500–549. Applicants are restricted to small loan amounts (up to
  INR 200,000) and short tenures (up to 24 months). Manual review required.

1.3 Recent Credit Events
Applications from individuals with any of the following within the past
24 months require mandatory REVIEW_REQUIRED classification:
- Loan defaults or settlements
- Written-off credit card accounts
- More than 3 delayed payments (>30 days)

1.4 Thin Credit File
Applicants with fewer than 3 credit accounts in their credit history are
considered to have a thin credit file. Such applications are routed to
REVIEW_REQUIRED regardless of score.

2. CO-APPLICANT POLICY

2.1 A co-applicant is mandatory when:
- The primary applicant's credit score is below 600
- The primary applicant is self-employed with less than 2 years in business
- The loan-to-income ratio exceeds 5x annual income

3. COLLATERAL POLICY

3.1 Unsecured loans (no collateral) are permitted only for:
- Salaried applicants with credit score >= 650
- Government employees regardless of credit score (minimum 550)
- Self-employed with >= 3 years in business and credit score >= 680

3.2 All other applicants must provide collateral for loans above INR 500,000.
